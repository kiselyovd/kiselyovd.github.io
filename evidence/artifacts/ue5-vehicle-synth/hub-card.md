# City Sample Vehicle Keypoints (24-point, synthetic)

[Capture explorer](https://kiselyovd.github.io/ue5-vehicle-synth/showcase/) · [Generator](https://github.com/kiselyovd/ue5-vehicle-synth) · [Transfer experiment](https://kiselyovd.github.io/vehicle-keypoints/phase0/synthetic-pretraining/)

[Live 14-point real-data detector](https://huggingface.co/spaces/kiselyovd/vehicle-keypoints-demo) is a separate model/application; it does not run the synthetic-only 24-point weights.


A synthetic vehicle-keypoint dataset rendered inside Epic's **City Sample**
("Matrix Awakens") with Unreal Engine 5.6 and Movie Render Queue. Vehicles are
placed on the real ZoneGraph road network, and every visible vehicle is labelled
with a 24-point anatomical keypoint schema plus a mesh-bounds bounding box.

Generated by the open pipeline at
[**kiselyovd/ue5-vehicle-synth**](https://github.com/kiselyovd/ue5-vehicle-synth)
(full method, code, and engineering write-up in the repo wiki).

[Source & capture workflow](https://github.com/kiselyovd/ue5-vehicle-synth) · [Transfer experiment](https://github.com/kiselyovd/vehicle-keypoints/blob/main/docs/phase0/synthetic-pretraining.md)

## Contents

- **2,510 frames** at 1280x720 PNG, across 36 capture groups: 4 city-street venues x 3 lighting presets (clear day, golden hour, overcast) x 3 hero vehicle rigs (sedan, minivan, pickup). Each scene is additionally populated with parked car/van types drawn at random from the City Sample fleet, so labelled instances cover more body shapes than the three hero rigs.
- **14,286 annotations** (multi-instance: the rig vehicle plus every visible labelled city vehicle per frame, ~5.7/frame), `annotations/coco.json` in COCO keypoint format.
- Per-point visibility split: 55.9% visible (v=2), 34.2% occluded (v=1), 9.9% off-frame/absent (v=0) - the capture is deliberately biased toward hard framing and occlusion.
- `captures_all.jsonl` - the raw per-frame records the COCO is built from.

## The 24-point schema

Points 0-13 are the **CarFusion canonical order** (4 wheels, 4 head/tail lights,
exhaust, 4 roof corners, body center) for backward compatibility with 14-point
models. Points 14-23 extend it: 2 side mirrors, 4 bumper corners, 4 window-base
corners. Per-point visibility follows CarFusion: `2` visible, `1` self-occluded,
`0` off-frame.

Every keypoint location is derived geometrically from the vehicle's own mesh data - wheels from the skeletal wheel bones, roof corners from the body vertex cloud's roofline plateau, head/tail lights and mirrors from the centroids of the mesh sections bound to the light/mirror materials - so per-type configs are exact to each body shape (a van's high tail lights, a pickup's cab-only roof), not a scaled sedan template.

## Intended use

Synthetic pre-training / augmentation for real-world vehicle-keypoint models
(sim-to-real). It is a **Phase-0 vertical slice**: the focus is the pipeline and
a proof-of-concept sample, not a final large-scale corpus.

## Used by

- [Synthetic 24-pt model](https://huggingface.co/kiselyovd/citysample-vehicle-keypoints-24pt) -
  a YOLO-pose model trained entirely on this synthetic data (the in-domain model).
- [Real CarFusion model](https://huggingface.co/kiselyovd/vehicle-keypoints) -
  the real-world baseline the sim-to-real study compares against.

## Evaluation status

The Phase 0 matched experiment is complete. Adding synthetic frames did not meet the **+2 pp OKS-mAP** criterion on held-out CarFusion: the supplement contributed **-1.7 pp** from the weak checkpoint and **-9.5 pp** from the strong one. This result applies to the tested 2D transfer recipe, independently of the synthetic-only in-domain benchmark. Read the [protocol and error analysis](https://github.com/kiselyovd/vehicle-keypoints/blob/main/docs/phase0/synthetic-pretraining.md).
